Temperature at 2 Meters: the selected models beat climatology on held-out 2018–2025 data at horizons 1, 2, 3, 4 month(s) (MAE skill up to 26%). How it works

Tested on years it never saw

The data

  • NASA POWER monthly, built on the MERRA-2 reanalysis.
  • 1981-01–2025-12 used for training.
  • 25 cells of 0.5° × 0.625° (about 55 km) over Bengaluru and its surroundings. One cell covers a whole city.
  • Forecast starts after December 2025, the last month acquired.

The models

  • Six candidates compete: Monthly average, Same month last year, Damped persistence, Average + trend, Ridge regression, Gradient boosting.
  • One is picked for each distance ahead, judged only on 2011–2017.
  • Scored on 2018-01–2024-12, which the choice never saw. Picked here: Damped persistence (recent anomalies that fade out toward the average); Monthly average (the long-term average for that calendar month).
  • Ranges are the 10th–90th and 2.5th–97.5th percentiles of the chosen model’s past misses.

What it can’t do

  • Intervals describe historical out-of-sample error of this statistical model; they are not climate-scenario uncertainty.
  • ~50 km cells: one cell covers all of Bengaluru; no neighbourhood-scale prediction.
  • Forecast issued from the last ACQUIRED month (Dec 2025); 2026 months already elapsed can be verified once acquired.
  • Not a weather forecast and not a climate projection.

Engine: aspect2729/bhuvyom · backend/engines/prediction · model version 1.0.0 · results generated 2026-11-13. Method basis: FPP3 (Hyndman & Athanasopoulos 2021); Tashman 2000; Lei et al. 2018. Actual 2026 values: Open-Meteo archive (ERA5), fetched live. Scenario warming: IPCC AR6 WGI Table SPM.1, central estimates. NASA POWER and ERA5 are different reanalyses, so small offsets between them are expected.

No rainfall scenario here

Monsoon rainfall depends on regional circulation that a simple warming scale cannot capture, so this page does not invent a rainfall projection. Switch to air temperature for the warming scenarios.
