HONEST DATA

TWO TEMPERATURES.
NEVER MIXED.

Air temperature is measured two metres above the ground. Land surface temperature is how hot the ground itself gets — roofs, roads, and open soils. They tell different stories, so they always appear as separate physical series.

HOW IT WORKS

FROM ORBIT TO STREET

  1. 01

    Retrieve

    Pull each environmental variable from NASA APIs across Karnataka's geographic bounding box.

  2. 02

    Process

    Normalize coordinates, clean gaps, calibrate against IMD stations, and preserve physical units.

  3. 03

    Visualise

    Ray-trace the 8K planet in WebGL2 and map street-level gradients across urban wards.

  4. 04

    Simulate

    Run what-if scenarios for cool roofs, lake restoration, and CMIP6 climate warming to 2100.

  5. 05

    Validate

    Audit ground truth sensors, uncertainty margins, and policy decision metrics.
